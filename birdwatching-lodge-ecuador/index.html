FAQ

Questions for planning the stay

Clear answers before booking a birding base.

Can I birdwatch directly from the retreat?

The property and surrounding landscape may offer bird activity from gardens, shared spaces and viewpoints, but sightings vary and are never guaranteed.

Are guided birdwatching tours included?

Do not assume a guide or transportation is included. Ask about current options, availability, pickup, timing and price before booking.

What is the best time of day for birding?

Early hours are often productive, but weather, habitat and season matter. Build flexibility into the plan and follow recent local guidance.

Which room is best for birdwatchers?

The best fit depends on space, outlook, schedule and sleeping arrangement. Compare the Panoramic Suite, Sunrise Room and Sunset Room, then confirm details directly.
