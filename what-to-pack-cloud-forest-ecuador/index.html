FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions based on current information.

What clothing should I pack for Ecuador’s cloud forest?

Pack breathable layers, a warm layer and rain protection. Adjust quantities and warmth to the current forecast, trip length and planned activities.

What shoes are best for the cloud forest?

Choose comfortable closed footwear with suitable traction for wet, uneven ground. Activity difficulty and current trail conditions should guide the final choice.

What should stay in my day bag?

Carry water, rain protection, sun protection, insect repellent, essential medication and dry storage for identification, phone and other personal items.

Can I assume outdoor equipment or toiletries are provided?

No. Amenities and activity equipment vary. Confirm what the property or provider supplies before deciding what to leave at home.
