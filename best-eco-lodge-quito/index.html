FAQ

Questions before booking

Clear answers for comparing the stay.

What makes an eco lodge near Quito a good fit?

A good fit combines a verified natural setting with suitable rooms, workable access, clear inclusions and an experience aligned with the group’s priorities.

Is The Cloud Forest Retreat certified as an eco lodge?

Do not infer a certification from descriptive language. Ask the retreat directly for any current certification, operating or sustainability information that matters to your decision.

What is included in the room price?

Inclusions can change. Confirm the room, meals, transportation, activities, guides, entrance fees, taxes, payment and cancellation terms for your dates.

How should I compare total value?

Compare the complete itinerary: room suitability, access, transport, meals, activity costs, time required and the setting you want—not the nightly rate alone.
