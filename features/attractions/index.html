FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions before you book.

Which attractions are closest to the retreat?

Pululahua, cloud forest routes and waterfall areas are among the regional options. Exact travel time and access depend on the route and current road conditions.

Can I visit Pululahua and a waterfall in one day?

It may be possible, but do not assume it is the best plan. Ask about travel times, daylight, weather and route conditions before combining outings.

Is transportation included?

Do not assume transportation is included with lodging. Confirm available arrangements, pickup details and current pricing with the host.

Do attractions require advance booking?

Some experiences or guides may require advance coordination. Confirm entry rules, providers, equipment and participant requirements for your dates.
