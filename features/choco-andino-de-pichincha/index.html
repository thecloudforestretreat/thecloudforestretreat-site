FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions before you book.

What is the Chocó Andino de Pichincha?

It is a biodiverse Andean region in Pichincha associated with cloud forests, watersheds and rural communities west and northwest of Quito.

Is the retreat inside the Chocó Andino landscape?

The retreat presents its location in the cloud forest landscape of Pichincha. Ask the host for precise local orientation and current access guidance for specific sites.

What wildlife will I see?

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Species activity varies with habitat, season, weather, time of day and chance.

How can I visit responsibly?

Stay on appropriate routes, minimize noise, avoid feeding wildlife, carry out waste and follow local guidance for protected or community-managed areas.
