FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions before you book.

Does the retreat have Wi-Fi?

Wi-Fi is listed among the retreat amenities. Ask about current availability and whether the connection is suitable for any work or calling needs you have.

Can guests use the kitchen?

There is a shared kitchen. Confirm current access, equipment, food storage and house rules before planning meals or shopping.

Are all rooms en suite?

No. The Sunrise and Sunset rooms share a full hallway bathroom. Review each room page for its current bathroom arrangement before choosing.

Is the retreat pet friendly?

Pet stays may be possible by request. Contact the host before booking to discuss the animal, dates, room choice and current conditions.
