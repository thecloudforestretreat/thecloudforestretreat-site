FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions before you book.

Where should I start?

Start with Amenities for practical comfort, Activities for ideas during the stay, and Nearby Attractions for outings beyond the property.

Are all activities available every day?

No. Availability can depend on weather, local conditions, advance arrangements and the experience provider. Confirm plans with the host for your dates.

Are activities included in the room rate?

Do not assume activities or transportation are included. Ask what the quoted rate covers and request current pricing for any additional arrangements.

Can the host help build an itinerary?

Yes. Share your dates, interests, pace and transportation needs so the host can suggest a realistic sequence and confirm what is currently available.
