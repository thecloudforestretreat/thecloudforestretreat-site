FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers before adding this landscape to your stay.

What is the Guayllabamba River?

It is a major river and watershed feature in Pichincha that connects valleys, slopes and downstream landscapes across the region.

Can guests walk directly to the river from the retreat?

Do not assume direct or casual access. Terrain can be steep and routes may cross private land, so ask the host for current guidance.

Can guests swim in the river?

Swimming should not be assumed safe or permitted. Flow, depth, water quality, access and weather can change; follow qualified local guidance.

Why does the river matter to a cloud forest stay?

The watershed helps explain the valley, elevation changes, moisture and connected habitats visible around the retreat.
