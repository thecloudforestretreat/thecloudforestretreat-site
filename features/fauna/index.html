FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions before you book.

What wildlife can guests see?

The region supports varied birdlife, butterflies and other animals. Exact sightings depend on habitat, season, weather, time and chance.

Are hummingbird sightings guaranteed?

No. Hummingbirds may be present, but activity changes. Plan observation for the experience rather than a guaranteed species checklist.

What is the best time for wildlife observation?

Early hours can be productive for birds, but the best timing varies. Ask the host about recent activity and local conditions.

How should I observe wildlife responsibly?

Keep a respectful distance, reduce noise, avoid feeding animals, stay on suitable routes and follow local guidance.
