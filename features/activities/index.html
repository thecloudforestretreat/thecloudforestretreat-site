FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions before you book.

Which activities are available at the retreat?

Quiet time outdoors and walking may be possible on or near the property. Yoga, biking, horseback riding and other outings require confirmation for your dates.

Do I need to book activities in advance?

Yes for arranged experiences. Contact the host early enough to confirm providers, timing, pricing, participant requirements and transportation.

What happens if the weather changes?

Cloud forest weather can change quickly. Routes and outdoor sessions may be adjusted, postponed or replaced with a safer option.

What should I bring?

Bring secure footwear and a light rain layer for outdoor plans. Ask about activity-specific clothing, equipment and safety requirements before arrival.
