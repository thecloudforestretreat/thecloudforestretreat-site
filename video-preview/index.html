How far is The Cloud Forest Retreat from Quito?

The retreat is about two hours from Quito, but travel time varies with your origin, traffic, weather, road conditions, and route.

What kind of traveler is the retreat best for?

It is especially well suited to couples, nature travelers, birders, wellness travelers, and guests who prefer a quieter private base.

Can you help with transportation and arrival planning?

Yes. Share your dates, starting point, group size, and approximate arrival time so practical options can be discussed.

How do I check current room availability?

Use the availability request or WhatsApp button. A host can confirm dates, room fit, current pricing, and the details needed to plan your stay.
