How far is The Cloud Forest Retreat from Quito?

The retreat is approximately two hours from Quito, depending on your starting point, route, and road conditions. We can help you plan transportation and arrival timing.

Is this a hotel or a private nature retreat?

The experience is closer to an intimate mountainside bed and breakfast and private nature retreat than a conventional city hotel. The emphasis is on scenery, comfort, quiet, and personal planning support.

Who is this cloud forest stay best for?

The stay works especially well for couples, nature travelers, birders, wellness travelers, and guests who want a peaceful escape within a broader Quito or Ecuador itinerary.

Can you help arrange transportation and activities?

Yes. Contact the retreat or use the WhatsApp widget to discuss possible dates, transportation, nearby activities, and the room that best fits your plans.

What should I pack for a cloud forest stay?

Bring comfortable layers, a light rain jacket, sturdy walking shoes, sun protection, and insect protection. Our complete packing guide explains how to prepare for changing conditions.
