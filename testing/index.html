Produce

Fresh, seasonal, and grown with care. What you eat here comes directly from the land around you.

At The Cloud Forest Retreat, much of what ends up on your plate begins just steps away. Our farm produces a rotating selection of fruits, vegetables, and herbs grown organically, shaped by the seasons, and harvested with intention.

From garden to table

Ingredients are picked at peak ripeness and used fresh. Meals here reflect what the land offers in that moment, creating a direct connection between soil, season, and flavor.

Picked daily Season-led menus Local flavor

How it works

  • Harvest in the morning for peak freshness.
  • Cook simple meals that let ingredients shine.
  • Rotate menus based on what is ready that week.

What you might see in season

  • Tree tomatoes, citrus, and seasonal fruits.
  • Peppers, leafy greens, herbs, and garden harvests.
  • Coffee cherries when conditions are right.

Seasonal highlights

Tree tomato

Tangy, vibrant, and often used in sauces and fresh preparations.

Peppers

Harvested weekly, adding warmth and depth to many dishes.

Fresh herbs

Picked moments before cooking for maximum aroma and flavor.

Eating here is not just about nourishment. It is about understanding where food comes from, honoring the land, and slowing down enough to taste it.
