Birdwatching in Ecuador stays in the memory

Nothing in the forest arrives all at once. Sound comes first, then movement, then shape and color as the leaves open for a moment. Mist shifts through the branches, light changes across the canopy, and each sighting feels tied to the place around it.

Damp air, filtered light, layered forest, and the feeling of staying inside the same atmosphere keep each moment close. The sightings never feel separate from the landscape that holds them.
