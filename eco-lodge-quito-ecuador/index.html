What “eco lodge” means for this stay

The value starts with place: a small lodging experience connected to the surrounding landscape rather than a city hotel or large resort.

We use the term to help travelers find a nature-focused stay. It does not imply a third-party environmental certification. Ask about the current operating practices, amenities and inclusions that matter to your decision.
