FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions based on current information.

What nature activities are available near Quito?

Options may include suitable hiking, bird observation, waterfall outings and quieter time in the landscape, but availability and access vary.

Are activities included with the stay?

Do not assume activities, guides, transport, entrance fees or equipment are included. Confirm each arrangement and price before booking around it.

Which activity is best for families or mixed abilities?

Choose according to the least mobile traveler, terrain, duration, weather, access and supervision needs. Request current details and plan conservatively.

What happens if weather changes?

Keep a lower-effort or more sheltered alternative. Rain, mist and road conditions may change access, safety, visibility and timing.
