Helpful answers

Common Areas questions

Practical answers about shared facilities and how they fit into your stay.

Are the common areas private to my room?

No. The living room, kitchen and common-area guest bathroom are shared spaces. Your bedroom is a separate choice; compare the room pages for its layout and bathroom arrangements.

Can guests use the kitchen?

The retreat has a shared kitchen. Confirm current access, available equipment, storage and any house rules with the host before planning to cook.

Are meals included with kitchen access?

Do not assume meals are included because there is a shared kitchen. Ask the host what your quoted rate includes and discuss meals and dietary needs for your dates.

Is the common-area bathroom the Sunrise and Sunset bathroom?

No. The common areas have a guest half bathroom. The Sunrise and Sunset rooms share a separate full bathroom in the hallway outside those bedrooms.
