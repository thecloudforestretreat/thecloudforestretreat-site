FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does sending a booking request confirm my stay?

No. A stay is confirmed only after availability, price, inclusions, payment requirements and the applicable conditions are confirmed.

Which cancellation policy applies?

Use the policy shown with your specific rate, room, dates and booking channel. Contact the retreat if anything is unclear before paying.

Do third-party booking platforms have separate terms?

Yes. A platform may apply its own account, payment, cancellation and privacy terms in addition to the confirmed terms for the stay.

What should I do if weather or road conditions change?

Contact the retreat for current guidance and make conservative travel decisions. Changes remain subject to the booking conditions and available alternatives.

Questions about these terms?

Contact us before booking if a condition, inclusion or responsibility is unclear.
