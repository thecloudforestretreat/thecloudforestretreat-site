FAQ

Questions before booking

Clear answers for comparing the stay.

Which room is best for a romantic getaway?

The best room depends on preferred space, outlook, sleeping arrangement and access. Compare all room pages and confirm current availability.

Are romantic packages or special services included?

Do not assume a package, decoration, meal, transportation or other service is included. Ask directly about current options, prices and advance notice.

Is the entire retreat private?

The site describes guest rooms and shared common areas. Confirm the room and space arrangement for your dates if privacy is a priority.

How many nights work well for couples?

Two nights can allow a calmer arrival, one full day together and an unhurried departure, but choose the length that fits the route and your plans.
