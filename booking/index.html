Questions before booking

Helpful booking details

Clear answers for the decisions travelers usually make before requesting availability.

Does this form confirm my reservation?

No. It starts a direct availability conversation. A reservation is confirmed only after the team verifies the details and you complete the agreed booking step.

Can I ask about transportation from Quito?

Yes. Include your starting point, approximate arrival time, and number of travelers so the team can discuss practical options.

What if I am unsure which room to choose?

Choose the option asking for a recommendation and explain what matters most to you. The team can clarify the room differences.

When will I receive arrival information?

Confirmed guests receive the location details and practical guidance needed to reach the retreat.
