FAQ

Whole-house rental questions

Direct answers for planning a private group stay.

Is the whole retreat reserved for one group?

Yes. When you reserve the whole-house stay, the Panoramic Suite, Sunrise Room, Sunset Room and the retreat's common areas are held together for your guest group. Confirm current inclusions and access before booking.

Which accommodations are included?

The whole-house option brings together the Panoramic Suite, Sunrise Room and Sunset Room. It also includes access to the common living and kitchen areas according to the arrangements confirmed for your stay.

What is the bathroom arrangement?

The Panoramic Suite has its own open bathroom arrangement with a bathtub by the panoramic windows. Sunrise Room and Sunset Room share a full hallway bathroom, and the common area also has a guest half bath.

How do I confirm capacity, rates and inclusions?

Send your dates, group size and needs through the booking form. The host will confirm current capacity, sleeping arrangements, rates, meals, access and what is included before you finalize the stay.

Can the retreat help with transportation from Quito?

Transportation help may be available. Share your arrival point, timing and group details so the retreat can confirm current options and practical route guidance.
