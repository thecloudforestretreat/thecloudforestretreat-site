FAQ

Questions for planning the stay

Clear answers before booking a birding base.

Can I see hummingbirds near Quito?

Hummingbirds occur in suitable Andean and cloud forest habitats, but activity and particular species vary with place, elevation, plants, weather, time and chance.

Are hummingbird sightings guaranteed at the retreat?

No. Birds move freely, and no species, number or viewing distance can be guaranteed for a stay.

Can I photograph hummingbirds at feeders or flowers?

Photography may be possible, but keep approach routes clear, avoid crowding, limit disturbance and follow current property or guide instructions.

What gear helps with hummingbird observation?

Binoculars, rain protection, layers and a camera configured before the encounter are useful. Protect electronics from moisture and avoid excessive equipment changes.
