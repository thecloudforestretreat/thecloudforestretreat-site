FAQ

Questions for a short escape

Clear answers for planning the weekend.

How many nights should I plan for a weekend getaway?

Two nights can create a comfortable rhythm: arrive and settle, keep one full day for the landscape, then leave with time for changing road or weather conditions.

How long does the trip from Quito take?

Travel time varies with your starting point, traffic, road choice and weather. Confirm the current route and departure timing directly before travel.

What can I do during a short stay?

Choose one or two priorities such as quiet time, bird observation, a confirmed nature outing or a nearby waterfall plan. Availability and conditions can change.

What should I confirm before booking?

Confirm room capacity, sleeping arrangements, meals, check-in timing, transportation needs, current inclusions and any activity arrangements.
