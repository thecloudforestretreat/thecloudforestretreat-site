FAQ

Planning questions

Clear answers for decisions based on current information.

Are waterfalls located directly at The Cloud Forest Retreat?

Do not assume a waterfall is on the property. Ask which sites are currently recommended, where they are and how access works.

Are transportation, guides and entrance fees included?

Inclusions vary. Confirm transportation, guiding, entry fees, equipment, meeting point and payment directly before the outing.

Are waterfall trails suitable for everyone?

Suitability depends on distance, surface, elevation, weather, river conditions and individual mobility. Request current difficulty details and choose conservatively.

Can heavy rain change the plan?

Yes. Rain can affect visibility, roads, trail surfaces, water levels and access. Keep a lower-effort alternative and follow current local guidance.
