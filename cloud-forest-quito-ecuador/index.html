FAQ

Questions before booking

Clear answers for comparing the stay.

Where is the cloud forest in relation to Quito?

The retreat is in the cloud forest landscape of Pichincha. Travel time varies with the exact starting point, route, traffic, weather and road conditions, so request current directions.

What is included in a cloud forest stay?

Inclusions can vary. Confirm the room, meals, transportation, activities, entrance fees and any guide or special service before booking.

When is the best time to visit?

Conditions vary across the year, and mist or rain can occur in any season. Choose dates according to your priorities and check current weather and access guidance.

Do I need to book nature activities in advance?

Some experiences may require transportation, a guide, access coordination or a separate fee. Ask what is available for your dates before relying on a specific plan.
