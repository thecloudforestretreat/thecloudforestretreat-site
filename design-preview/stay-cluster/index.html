Close enough for an easy escape. Immersive enough to feel elsewhere.

The Cloud Forest Retreat is designed for travelers who want the landscape to matter as much as the room. It is a calm base for couples, nature travelers, birders, and anyone looking for a restorative stay beyond a standard city hotel.

Wake to changing mountain light, follow the mist across the valley, and give yourself room to plan less. The experience is intentionally simple: comfortable spaces, direct access to nature, and personal help when you need it.

Questions before you stay

Helpful details for planning

Start with the practical questions travelers ask most often when planning a stay in the cloud forest.

How far is the retreat from Quito?

Travel time depends on your starting point and road conditions. Contact us with your dates and arrival plans so we can help you plan the most practical route.

Is this a hotel or a private retreat?

The experience is closer to a private nature stay than a conventional hotel. The emphasis is on space, scenery, comfort, and a more personal pace.

Can you help with transportation and activities?

Yes. Use the contact form or WhatsApp widget to ask about transport and the activities that best fit your schedule.

What should I arrange before arriving?

Confirm your room, arrival plans, transportation needs, and any activities that require advance coordination before traveling to the retreat.
