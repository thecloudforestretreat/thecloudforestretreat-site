Questions before booking

Helpful booking details

Short answers for the decisions travelers usually make before sending a request.

Does this form confirm my reservation?

No. It begins a direct availability conversation. Your reservation is confirmed only after the team verifies the details and you complete the agreed booking step.

Can I ask about transportation from Quito?

Yes. Include your starting point, approximate arrival time, and number of travelers so the team can discuss practical options.

What if I am unsure which room to choose?

Select “Recommend a room” and share what matters most to you. The team can explain the differences before you decide.

When will I receive arrival information?

Confirmed guests receive the location details and practical arrival guidance needed to reach the retreat with confidence.
