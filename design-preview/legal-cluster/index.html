Plain-language introduction

What these terms are designed to clarify.

These terms explain the relationship between the retreat and the people who use this website, request information, or reserve a stay. Specific booking conditions shown during confirmation also apply to that reservation.

01

Scope and acceptance

By using this website or submitting a reservation request, you agree to use the site lawfully and provide accurate information. A request is not a confirmed reservation until the retreat provides confirmation.

02

Reservations and payment

Availability, pricing, occupancy, included services, payment schedules, and any stay-specific conditions should be confirmed before payment.

  • Review the room, dates, occupancy, and included services.
  • Use the payment method and schedule provided with confirmation.
  • Contact the retreat promptly if any reservation information is incorrect.
03

Changes and cancellations

The cancellation and change conditions supplied with the booking confirmation govern the reservation. Refund eligibility can depend on timing, rate conditions, third-party provider terms, and costs already incurred.

04

Guest responsibilities

Guests are responsible for respectful conduct, following safety and property guidance, supervising minors, disclosing relevant accessibility needs, and respecting the surrounding community and environment.

05

Website information and intellectual property

We work to keep property details, photography, pricing context, and travel information accurate. Conditions can change, and current confirmation details take priority over general website content.

Unless otherwise indicated, the website’s written content, branding, original photography, and design elements belong to The Cloud Forest Retreat and may not be republished without permission.

06

Questions and contact

If any term is unclear or you need the conditions for a specific reservation, contact the retreat before making a payment or beginning your journey.
