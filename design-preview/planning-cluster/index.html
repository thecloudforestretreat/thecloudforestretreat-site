Helpful before you travel

Answers that make arrival feel easier

Current guidance should always take priority over assumptions made early in the trip.

How long does the journey from Quito take?

Travel time varies with your starting point, traffic, weather, route, and current road conditions.

Can you help arrange transportation?

Share your dates, group size, starting point, and approximate arrival time so practical options can be discussed.

What weather should I prepare for?

Cloud forest conditions can change quickly. Layers, rain protection, and suitable footwear are useful throughout the year.

Should activities be planned before arrival?

Anything requiring transportation, a guide, or advance coordination is best discussed before your stay.
