Contact questions

Before you send a message

Quick answers about response, reservations, transportation, and languages.

Can I reserve through the contact form?

For availability, dates, and room requests, use the booking form. The contact form is best for general questions and planning help.

Can I ask about transportation from Quito?

Yes. Share your starting point, approximate timing, and group size so the team can discuss practical options.

Can I write in Spanish?

Yes. The retreat welcomes messages in English or Spanish.

Where is the exact property location?

The retreat is in Pichincha, approximately two hours from Quito. Confirmed guests receive exact location and arrival guidance.
