Fauna

From colorful aves to elusive mammals, the bosque nublado surrounding The Cloud Forest Retreat is alive with movement, sound, and rare encounters.

The Chocó Andino región is one of the richest wildlife habitats in the world. Birds dominate the skies and canopy, while mammals like the spectacled bear quietly roam forest corridors below.
